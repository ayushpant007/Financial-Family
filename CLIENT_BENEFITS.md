# Financial Family — For Our Clients

> One simple place to see everything you own, everything you owe, and what you're truly worth — updated in real time.

---

## The problem most people face

Managing your money today means juggling **a dozen different apps and statements**:

- Mutual funds in one app
- Stocks in another
- FDs hidden in bank emails
- PPF passbooks at the post office
- Loans tracked separately
- And you still have **no idea** what your real net worth is *today*

You only find out where you really stand when your advisor sends a quarterly PDF — by which time the numbers are already old.

---

## What Financial Family gives you

A **single, always-current view** of your entire financial life — built and maintained by your trusted advisor, available to you anytime.

### Everything in one place
See all your wealth at a glance — mutual funds, stocks, fixed deposits, recurring deposits, PPF/EPF, and bank balances — alongside your loans, all on one screen.

### Real, live numbers
- Mutual fund values update with **today's NAV**
- Stock holdings show **today's market price**
- FDs, RDs, and PPF show **interest earned till today** with a clear breakdown of how it was calculated

### Your net worth, instantly
No more guessing. The moment you log in, you see:
- **Total Assets** — what you own
- **Total Liabilities** — what you owe
- **Net Worth** — the number that actually matters

### Visual portfolio breakdown
A simple chart shows exactly how your money is split across mutual funds, stocks, deposits, and more — so you can see if you're too heavy in one place.

### Transparent gain/loss
Every stock and mutual fund shows you exactly how much you've gained or lost — in rupees and as a percentage — since you bought it.

### Your advisor does the heavy lifting
You don't have to enter or update anything. Your financial advisor keeps your portfolio current. **You just log in and see.**

### Private and secure
Only you can see your portfolio. Your data is protected with secure logins and stays with your trusted advisor — no marketing, no upselling, no third parties.
